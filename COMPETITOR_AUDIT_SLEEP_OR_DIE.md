## 💡 WHAT KYNE CAN LEARN

### 1. **Packaging Simplicity**
Sleep or Die's black matte pouches are clean and premium. KYNE should consider:
- Individual foil pouches (not a bottle)
- Bold typography on packaging
- Less is more - don't clutter with health claims

### 2. **Voice & Tone**
Sleep or Die's irreverent voice cuts through wellness-industry BS. KYNE's tone is more refined, but could add:
- More personality in copy
- Self-aware humor (own the "protocol" clinical vibe)
- Relatable language alongside science

### 3. **Speed Positioning**
"10 minutes, not 45" is a killer claim. KYNE already says "90 seconds" - lean into it harder:
- "Your supplements in 90 seconds. Coffee takes longer."
- Comparison: Capsules (30 min) vs KYNE strips (90 sec)

### 4. **Subscription Strategy**
Sleep or Die makes subscriptions the hero:
- 3 tiers (10-20% off)
- "Pause, edit, or cancel anytime" reduces friction
- "Never have the panic 'I am almost out' moment"

KYNE should:
- Make subscription the default (one-time is secondary)
- Show $ saved per year ("Save $240/year")
- Add "pause or skip" messaging

### 5. **Community Language**
"Zombies" creates identity. KYNE could have:
- "Protocol people" or "KYNE crew"
- User-generated content: "From our protocol people"
- Inside language that creates belonging

3. **Functional Benefits**
   - **Light-blocking** - protects active ingredients
   - **Moisture-proof** - keeps strips fresh
   - **Tamper-evident** - you can tell if opened
   - **Travel-friendly** - throw 3-4 in your pocket/purse

4. **Brand Consistency**
   - Box = matte/subtle
   - Strip pouches = shiny/eye-catching
   - Creates visual rhythm (contrast)
