# KYNE MASS PRODUCTION PACKAGING CONCEPTS

**Goal:** Affordable, scalable, unique enough to differentiate from Sleep or Die

---

## 💰 COST-EFFECTIVE BUT UNIQUE IDEAS

### CONCEPT 1: STANDING POUCH (Zip-Lock Style)
**The Big Idea:** Ditch the box entirely. Use a standing pouch like coffee/protein powder.

**Execution:**
- Matte black standing pouch (resealable zip-lock top)
- Foil-lined interior (keeps strips fresh)
- Bold white typography printed on front
- "KYNE / protocol / CORE · MORNING"
- Small window cutout showing foil strips inside
- Hangs or stands on shelf

**Why It Works:**
- ✅ CHEAP: $0.30-0.50 per pouch (vs $1.40 for rigid box)
- ✅ Flexible = less shipping cost
- ✅ Resealable = functional
- ✅ Standing pouch = premium coffee brand vibe
- ✅ Easy to manufacture anywhere

**Differentiation from Sleep or Die:** They use rigid box, you use pouch (coffee brand aesthetic)

**Cost:** $0.30-0.50 per unit

---

### CONCEPT 2: SLEEVE OVER TRAY (Matchbox Style)
**The Big Idea:** Inner tray slides out of outer sleeve (like a matchbox)

**Execution:**
- Outer sleeve: Printed card stock with bold design
- Inner tray: Simple white tray holding foil pouches
- Sleeve slides off to reveal tray
- Cheap to produce (just folded cardboard)
- Can print bold graphics on sleeve

**Why It Works:**
- ✅ CHEAP: $0.50-0.70 per unit
- ✅ Satisfying unboxing (slide mechanism)
- ✅ Two print surfaces (sleeve + tray)
- ✅ Easy to manufacture (standard folding carton)
- ✅ Compact, ships flat

**Differentiation:** Matchbox mechanism = tactile, playful

**Cost:** $0.50-0.70 per unit

---

### CONCEPT 3: ENVELOPE/MAILER STYLE (Flat Pack)
**The Big Idea:** Flat envelope that opens like mail (sustainable, minimal)

**Execution:**
- Thick paper envelope (like luxury stationery)
- Tear-open perforated edge
- Foil strips inside in a row
- Printed on front like a postcard
- "You've got mail" unboxing moment
- Flat = low shipping cost

**Why It Works:**
- ✅ CHEAP: $0.20-0.40 per unit (just paper)
- ✅ Sustainable (flat = less waste)
- ✅ Novel format (nobody does envelopes for supplements)
- ✅ Easy to manufacture
- ✅ Ships as flat as possible

**Differentiation:** Envelope = like receiving a letter

**Cost:** $0.20-0.40 per unit

---

### CONCEPT 4: VERTICAL STRIP DISPENSER (Like Tic-Tacs)
**The Big Idea:** Plastic dispenser that flips open, strips dispense one-by-one

**Execution:**
- Small vertical plastic case (like Tic-Tac container)
- Flip-top lid
- Strips stacked vertically inside in foil pouches
- Shake to dispense one strip
- Pocket-sized, portable
- Refillable (sell refill packs)

**Why It Works:**
- ✅ CHEAP: $0.60-0.90 per unit (injection molded plastic)
- ✅ Functional (dispenses easily)
- ✅ Reusable (buy refills)
- ✅ Portable (pocket-sized)
- ✅ Familiar format (Tic-Tacs, Altoids)

**Differentiation:** Dispenser mechanism = functional innovation

**Cost:** $0.60-0.90 per unit (initial), $0.30 for refills

---

### CONCEPT 5: PAPER TUBE (Like Pringles but Premium)
**The Big Idea:** Cardboard tube with metal ends (like tennis balls or premium chips)

**Execution:**
- Cardboard tube body (printed with design)
- Metal or thick cardboard end caps
- Foil strips roll inside vertically
- Label wrap around tube
- Stands vertically or lays flat
- Eco-friendly (recyclable cardboard)

**Why It Works:**
- ✅ CHEAP: $0.40-0.60 per unit
- ✅ Unique shape (cylinders stand out on shelf)
- ✅ Sustainable (cardboard tube)
- ✅ Easy to manufacture
- ✅ Strong structure (doesn't crush)

**Differentiation:** Tube = different shelf presence than boxes

**Cost:** $0.40-0.60 per unit

---

### CONCEPT 6: COLOR-CODED SIMPLE BOX (Sleep or Die Format + Twist)
**The Big Idea:** Use Sleep or Die's black matte format BUT add vibrant color accent

**Execution:**
- Black matte box (same as Sleep or Die)
- BUT: One bold accent color stripe/panel per product
  * Morning = Bright yellow stripe
  * Day = Electric teal stripe  
  * Night = Deep indigo stripe
- Same quality as Sleep or Die
- Differentiated by COLOR SYSTEM

**Why It Works:**
- ✅ Proven format (Sleep or Die validates it)
- ✅ Differentiated by color (they're all black/white)
- ✅ Easy to manufacture (standard boxes)
- ✅ Color = instant product recognition

**Differentiation:** Bold color accents (vs Sleep or Die's monochrome)

**Cost:** $0.80-1.00 per unit (same as Sleep or Die but with color printing)
