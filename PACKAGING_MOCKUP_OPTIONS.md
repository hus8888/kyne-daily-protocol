## 🎯 MY TOP 3 RECOMMENDATIONS

### #1: BLACK MATTE + GOLD FOIL (Original recommendation)
**Why it works:**
- ✅ Matches Sleep or Die format (black matte)
- ✅ Elevates with gold (more premium)
- ✅ Gender-neutral (not too masculine/feminine)
- ✅ Instagram-worthy (high contrast)
- ✅ Retail shelf presence (bold)

**Best for:** Luxury wellness, biohackers, longevity crowd

---

### #2: WHITE MATTE + BLACK TEXT
**Why it works:**
- ✅ Differentiates from Sleep or Die (white vs black)
- ✅ Clean/medical aesthetic (trust signal)
- ✅ Less common in supplement space (stands out)
- ✅ Timeless (won't look dated)
- ✅ Shows confidence (not hiding behind dark colors)

**Best for:** Science-first crowd, minimalists, Apple fans

---

### #3: CHARCOAL GREY + ROSE GOLD
**Why it works:**
- ✅ Softer than pure black (more approachable)
- ✅ Rose gold = modern luxury (not traditional)
- ✅ Appeals to female market (without being stereotypical)
- ✅ Differentiated from competitors
- ✅ Instagram aesthetic (warm metals trending)

**Best for:** Wellness lifestyle, beauty crossover, female-leaning
